# Students-Migration-Analyst-Project
Global Student Mobility Report 

## Project Overview

This project uses Python to explore global student mobility. It examines students’ countries of origin and destination, scholarship distribution, reasons for choosing a study destination, and employment outcomes.

## Objectives

- Identify the most popular destination countries for students.
- Explore why students choose to study in the UAE.
- Identify the main countries of origin.
- Examine scholarship distribution by country of origin.
- Compare CGPA scores for students who secured placements and those who did not.
- Compare average starting salaries across placement countries.
- Explore how starting salaries vary by field of study.
- Investigate student movement between countries of origin and study destinations.

## Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Data Cleaning and Preparation

The dataset was reviewed and prepared for analysis. This included:

- Checking for missing values and handling them where appropriate.
- Identifying duplicate records.
- Reviewing data types and numerical columns.
- Preparing the dataset for analysis and visualisation.

## Analysis and Visualisation

1. **Student destination countries:** Identified the most common study destinations in the dataset.
2. **Reasons for choosing the UAE:** Explored students’ recorded reasons for selecting the UAE.
3. **Countries of origin:** Identified the countries with the highest numbers of students.
4. **Scholarship distribution:** Compared scholarship records across countries of origin.
5. **CGPA and placement status:** Compared the academic scores of students who secured placements with those who did not.
6. **Placement country and starting salary:** Examined average starting salaries across placement countries.
7. **Field of study and starting salary:** Compared average starting salaries across different fields of study.
8. **Student mobility:** Explored movement patterns by comparing students’ countries of origin with their study destinations.

## Key Findings

The analysis explores student destination preferences, reasons for choosing the UAE, countries of origin, scholarship distribution, academic performance, and employment outcomes. Specific findings and numerical comparisons can be added once the analysis results have been reviewed.
